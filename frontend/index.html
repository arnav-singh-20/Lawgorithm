What the contract says

“5. SUMMARY RE-ENTRY. The Landlord may enter and repossess the premises without any court proceedings if rent is delayed by more than 15 days.”

Residential lease · Bengaluru
→
What it actually means 🚩 Red flag

If you’re more than 15 days late with rent, the landlord says they can take the house back without going to court.

The law behind it

§ Karnataka Rent Act, 1999 · s.27 Tenants are protected from eviction except through the legal process.

→

What you should do
Ask for this clause to be removed, or changed so any eviction follows the court process.

How it works

Built to never bluff.

Most AI will confidently make up a law. Lawgorithm checks itself at every step — and tells you when it isn’t sure.

  1. 01

    It reads everything

    Your PDF or photo is turned into text and split into clauses, then summarised in simple words.

  2. 02

    It looks up the law

    For each clause it searches real Indian statutes — the Contract Act, rent laws and more — not its memory.

  3. 03

    It checks its own work

    Every legal claim must point to a real section that actually says it. Anything shaky goes to a human.

  4. 04

    You get a game plan

    Red, amber or green for every clause, what it means, and exactly what to ask for before you sign.

Questions people ask

Is this a lawyer?

No. Lawgorithm explains contracts in plain words and shows the law behind each point. For anything marked red, talk to a lawyer before you sign.

Which contracts can it read?

Rental and job contracts from India — as a PDF, a Word file or just a photo.

Is it free?

Yes. It runs on free AI capacity, so on very busy days it may ask you to try again later.

What happens to my document?

It’s read in memory, never saved, and erased within 60 minutes. Phone numbers, PAN, Aadhaar and bank details are removed before any AI sees the text.

How sure is it?

Every legal claim must point to a real section of law, and a second check confirms the law actually says it. When it isn’t sure, it tells you instead of guessing.
