# Lawgorithm

Upload an Indian rental or employment contract (PDF or photo) and get every
clause explained in simple words, marked red / amber / green, with the Indian
law behind it, in 7 languages.

**Privacy (DPDP Act, 2023):** documents are processed in memory only and never
stored. Phone numbers, emails, PAN, Aadhaar and bank details are removed
before the text is sent to the AI provider (Groq), and results are erased
automatically after 60 minutes. Not legal advice.
